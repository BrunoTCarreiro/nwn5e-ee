# 16000120-16000121: Second Wind (Fighter F2).
16000120 Second Wind
16000121 Second Wind (Fighter, 2024): regain 1d10 + your Fighter level hit points. Uses: 2 (3 from Fighter 4, 4 from 10), back on a rest. In the 2024 rules this is a Bonus Action; until nwn5e has bonus actions (Phase 6) it takes your action.\n\nFrom Fighter 5 (Tactical Shift), for the rest of the round you can move without provoking opportunity attacks.

16000270 Species (2024): Dwarf.\n\nDarkvision: 120 feet.\nDwarven Resilience: resistance to Poison damage, and advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Poisoned condition.\nDwarven Toughness: your hit point maximum increases by 1 per level.\nStonecunning: as a Bonus Action, tremorsense (60 feet) for 10 minutes while you are on stone, a few times per Long Rest.\n\nIn nwn5e now: advantage on saving throws against poison. The other traits come with the species rules.
16000271 Species (2024): Elf.\n\nDarkvision: 60 feet.\nElven Lineage: Drow, High Elf or Wood Elf, each with a cantrip at level 1 and a spell at levels 3 and 5 (Darkvision 120 feet for the Drow, Speed 35 feet for the Wood Elf).\nFey Ancestry: advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed condition.\nKeen Senses: proficiency in Insight, Perception or Survival.\nTrance: you don't need sleep; a Long Rest takes 4 hours of meditation.\n\nIn nwn5e now: advantage on saving throws against mind spells (Fey Ancestry), and Keen Senses (pick it with !skills). The lineages come with spells, Trance with rests.
16000272 Species (2024): Gnome.\n\nDarkvision: 60 feet.\nGnomish Cunning: advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.\nGnomish Lineage: Forest Gnome (the Minor Illusion cantrip, and Speak with Animals a few times per Long Rest) or Rock Gnome (the Mending and Prestidigitation cantrips, and tiny clockwork devices).\n\nIn nwn5e now: advantage on Wisdom saving throws (the game's Will saves; it has no Intelligence or Charisma saves). The lineages come with spells.
16000273 Species (2024): Halfling.\n\nBrave: advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Frightened condition.\nHalfling Nimbleness: you can move through the space of a creature a size larger than you.\nLuck: when you roll a 1 on the d20 of an attack roll, ability check or saving throw, you reroll it and must use the new roll.\nNaturally Stealthy: you can hide even when only a creature at least one size larger than you obscures you.\n\nIn nwn5e now: Brave (advantage on saving throws against fear) and Luck (on attack rolls, saving throws and initiative).
16000274 Legacy species: Half-Elf.
