Recipe

0 = the base as it is, 1 = the mix, below 0 pushes the base further away from the mix.

The recipe file

Put the file in build\animedit\recipes\ (a .recipe.txt works as it is). nwn5e builds it into your character's animations the next time the game starts; !animedit or !anim then plays it.

Load your animation libraries

  1. Start the game once with nwn5e 0.24.2 or later: it copies its animation libraries to build\animedit\libraries\.
  2. Choose or drop those .mdl files on the left. a_ba.mdl with a_ba_non_combat.mdl and a_ba_med_weap.mdl covers men of most races; a_fa, a_da and a_dfa are the other bodies.
  3. Pick a base and a mix. The three figures play the base, the recipe and the mix. Drag to turn them, scroll to zoom.
